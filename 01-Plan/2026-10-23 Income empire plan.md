# acTS income empire plan

All Creative Technology Solutions (acTS) · 23 Oct 2026 · Jess + Claude (Chief of Staff)

## Bottom line

Starting with zero capital means paying with your time instead of money, and nothing is passive on day one. We build the income streams in an order that pays for itself. Services bring in cash within weeks. The tools site, digital products and affiliate links take 3–12 months to build up, and after that they need only a few hours a month. Everything below starts for under AU$100 (the domain plus the business name). Nothing else gets spent until a stream has earned the money.

**The biggest unused asset is the acTS Tools site.** It has 154 working, tested tools and earns nothing because it isn't live. It also has one blocker: tools open at `#/slug` links (`public/assets/js/app.js`), so Google sees one page instead of 154. Each tool needs its own real URL before search traffic, and the ad money that comes with it, can arrive.

## Order of play

### Days 1–30: cash and foundations

- **Jess:** buy the domain, register the business name (ASIC, $47 a year), and open a free business bank account. These unblock streams 2, 3 and 4: ad networks, stores and affiliate programs all want a live site.
- **Jess:** sign up for Payhip (free plan) and connect Stripe. Payhip delivers the files, and collects and pays EU and UK VAT and US and Canadian sales tax. Australian buyers pay no GST while acTS isn't registered. Stripe also handles service invoices.
- **Jess:** send the family creator questions and ask each creator for two introductions. Aim for the first one or two paid clients (stream 1).
- **Claude (tools agents):** give each tool its own URL, add a "Want this done for you?" link to acTS, and put the tools site live (stream 2).
- **empire-maker:** Creator Security Pack v1, which Jess reviews before it's listed (stream 3).
- **empire-scout:** check the affiliate programs and what Australian clippers and setup builders charge.

### Days 31–90: switch on the low-effort streams

- Apply for AdSense once the site has real pages and some traffic. It needs the privacy policy and a consent message for EU and UK visitors (AdSense provides one free).
- Join the scout's first five programs: RØDE (15%), Proton (30%, renewals included), Bitdefender (20%), Descript (US$25 per new subscriber) and Amazon Associates AU (5% on cameras and audio, 2% on computers). Join Amazon last, once a guide is live, because it closes accounts without 3 sales in the first 180 days.
- Amazon bans its links in PDFs and ebooks, so the setup guides need web versions to carry them. A PDF can link to the acTS web page instead.
- Two more products: setup guides by budget, and a media kit with a rate card template.
- Start acTS short-form: 3 posts a week, cut from the work itself (setup builds, security tips).

### Months 4–12: let it build

- Monthly: the scout finds the next tools worth adding (search demand) and the tools agents build them.
- Turn on the AI writing pro tier once AI writing is running.
- Revisit the creator dashboard once 3 or more paying clients can be its first users.

## Rules the empire runs on

1. **Self-funding.** No spending until a stream has earned the money, except the domain and the business name.
2. **Every dollar goes in the ledger** (`05-Finance/Empire/ledger.csv`). Tax time and the scoreboard both need it.
3. **Kill rule.** If a stream has no income and no growing traffic after 6 months, cut it or change it.
4. **Disclose** every affiliate link and paid relationship, next to the link, in plain words: "Ad: we earn a commission if you buy through these links, at no extra cost to you." The word "affiliate" on its own may not be enough for the ACCC and the advertising code. Pages with Amazon links also need Amazon's exact line: "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases."
5. **Agents research, draft and track. Jess signs up, pays, posts, publishes and sends.** No agent creates accounts, spends money or posts.

## Not doing, and why

- **Dropshipping or paid ads:** need capital and can lose it fast.
- **Trading, crypto or forex:** not low risk, and not something Claude advises on.
- **Mass AI-written sites or books:** Google and Amazon penalise them, and they earn little.
- **Social media downloaders:** clash with acTS's official-APIs-only stance.
- **"How to make money" courses and MLM:** a reputation risk for a business whose brand depends on trust.
