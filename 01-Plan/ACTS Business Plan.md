# ACTS Business Plan

All Creative Technology Solutions · Sydney, NSW

Oct 19, 2026 · @Jess

## The plan

ACTS handles the tech side for content creators so they can stay focused on the creative side. We get there in five steps, each finished and agreed before the next starts.

1. **Starter offer.** Narrow the service ideas to two or three that cost nothing to start, and test them with family creators.
2. **Business plan and model.** Customers, offer and pricing, a simple financial model with break-even, and the NSW setup (sole trader, ABN, business name, GST, insurance, tools).
3. **Brand definition.** Name use, personality, voice, visual style and example copy, written as files any Claude agent can read and follow exactly.
4. **Launch kit.** A one-page site or link page, a portfolio from pilot work with family creators, and outreach messages for the first paying clients, all built from the brand files. Website tasks: [2026-10-19 Website to-do list](2026-10-19%20Website%20to-do%20list.md).
5. **How we run it.** Who does what between Jess and Claude, and a weekly routine for clients, work and money.

## Step 1: Starter offer

Start with three services: beginner setup builds, clipping, and safety and security packs. Together they cost almost nothing to launch, use Jess's strongest skills, and mix one-off fees, monthly retainers and a product that sells repeatedly. Ads and analytics come next, once clipping clients and a portfolio exist.

| Service | What the creator gets | Upfront cost | Income type | Claude's part | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner setup builds | A budget parts list for camera, mic, lighting and PC, plus remote or in-person setup help | None | One-off fee, with upsells | Price research, parts lists, setup guides | Start now |
| Podcast and long-form clipping | Short clips cut from long videos, ready to post | Free editing software to start | Monthly retainer | Finding clip moments from transcripts, captions, titles | Start now |

| Content analytics | Monthly report on what is working and what to post next | None | Monthly add-on | Reading exports and writing the report | Add-on for clipping clients |

Prices are set in step 2. Upfront costs here are an estimate, not a quote.

## Step 1: Questions for family creators

Ask each family creator these questions in a 15-minute chat or a message. Their answers decide which services we lead with and what to charge in step 2.

**About them**

1. What platforms do you post on, and how often?
2. Roughly how many followers or subscribers do you have on your main platform?
3. Is creating content a hobby, a side income, or your main income?

**Where the tech gets in the way**

4. Which part of making content takes the most time that isn't the creative part?
5. What tech problem has cost you a stream, an upload or a deal?
6. If someone took one task off your plate tomorrow, which would it be?

**The services**

7. Have you ever paid for gear advice, editing or clipping? What did it cost, and was it worth it?
8. Would you pay for clips cut from your long videos every month? How many clips a week would be useful?
9. Do you know how to protect your accounts from hackers, scams and doxxing? Would a short guide or a training session help?
10. Have you ever made an ad, for yourself or a sponsor? Would you pay someone to make one?
11. Do you check your analytics? What would you want a monthly report to tell you?

**Money and trust**

12. What would you expect to pay per month for help with the tech side?
13. What would make you trust someone with your accounts or footage?
14. Do you know other creators who would want this? Would you be happy to introduce us?
