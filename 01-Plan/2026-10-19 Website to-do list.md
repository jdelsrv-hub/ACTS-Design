# acTS website: to-do list

All Creative Technology Solutions (acTS) · part of step 4 (launch kit) · drafted 19 Oct 2026

The goal is a simple, low-cost site that tells a creator what we do, shows we're legit and gets them to book a free 15-minute chat. It starts as one page and grows as the portfolio grows.

The logged-in creator dashboard and tools are planned separately in the [dashboard and tools plan](2026-10-20%20Dashboard%20and%20tools%20plan.md). That work depends on this list: the platforms' app approvals need this site live on our own domain with a privacy policy, so phases 2 and 4 here come first.

**Who:** J = Jess, C = Claude, J+C = together. Costs are estimates in AUD; check before paying.

## Recommended setup (lowest cost)

| Piece | Pick | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website | One-page site Claude builds from the brand kit, hosted free on Cloudflare Pages or Netlify | Free |
| Domain | A `.com.au` name (needs an ABN) | About $15–30 a year |
| Email | `hello@` our domain, forwarded free to Jess's Gmail (Cloudflare Email Routing), or Zoho Mail's free plan | Free |
| Bookings | Cal.com or Calendly free plan for "Book a free 15-minute chat" | Free |
| Contact form | Tally free plan | Free |
| Visitor stats | Cloudflare Web Analytics (no cookie banner needed) | Free |

Total to launch: roughly the cost of the domain. If Jess would rather edit the site by drag and drop, Carrd Pro (about US$19 a year) is the fallback.

## Phase 1: Things that must happen first

- [ ] **J:** Review and sign off the brand kit, especially colours and logo (`02-Brand/`). The site is built straight from it.
- [ ] **J:** Get an ABN (free at abr.gov.au). A `.com.au` domain needs one.

- [ ] **J:** Run the family creator questions. Their answers decide which service leads on the home page.

## Phase 2: Name and accounts

- [ ] **C:** Shortlist available domains (for example `acts.com.au`, `actscreative.com.au`, `actstech.com.au`) and matching social handles.
- [ ] **J:** Buy the domain once the ABN arrives (VentraIP, Crazy Domains or Cloudflare). Turn on auto-renew.
- [ ] **J:** Make free accounts: Cloudflare (or Netlify), Cal.com or Calendly, Tally. Use a strong password and two-step login on each, as we'd tell a client to.
- [ ] **J+C:** Set up `hello@` email forwarding and test it both ways.
- [ ] **J:** Claim matching handles on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Twitch, even if unused for now.

## Phase 3: Content

- [ ] **C:** Write the page copy from `04-copy-library.md`: hero ("You create. We sort the tech."), the three starter services, how it works (chat, plan, sorted), about us, FAQ, and contact.
- [ ] **J:** Edit the copy so it sounds like us. Add anything only Jess knows (experience, gear you love, a short personal line).
