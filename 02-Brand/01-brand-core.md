# 01 · Brand Core

## Who we are

acTS (All Creative Technology Solutions) is a small Sydney studio that handles the tech side of content creation, so creators can stay on the creative side. We build budget-smart setups, clip long-form content, make ads, read the analytics, and teach creators to stay safe online.

## Mission

Make the tech behind content creation easy, so every creator can focus on what they make.

## The promise

Every creator who works with us feels **supported and heard**. If something is outside what we do, we don't leave them stuck: we help them find the right solution or service.

## Who we serve

**Primary:** new and growing creators who want to create, not troubleshoot.

- A TikTok creator who wants to start live streaming and doesn't know what gear or software they need.
- A podcaster or long-form creator who wants their videos clipped into short-form content.

**What they're feeling:** excited about creating, overwhelmed by gear, settings and platforms, worried about wasting money or getting hacked, and short on time.

**What they want from us:** someone who explains things simply, recommends what's actually worth buying, does the fiddly work for them, and is there when things break.

## Personality

| Trait | What it means | What it is not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Easy** | We remove steps, jargon and stress. Every answer leads to a clear next step. | Dumbed down or patronising |
| **Useful** | Practical advice that fits the creator's budget and goals. We show, not just tell. | Vague inspiration or hype |
| **Intuitive** | Things just make sense: our messages, guides and setups follow the order a creator thinks in. | Clever for the sake of it |
| **Calm expert** | We know our stuff and stay steady when a stream crashes at 9pm. | Cold, corporate or know-it-all |
| **A bit bold** | We have opinions ("skip that mic, get this one") and a confident, modern look. | Loud, edgy or try-hard |

## Values

1. **Creator first.** The creator's goals and budget come before any upsell.
2. **Straight talk.** Honest recommendations, clear prices, no hidden catches.
3. **Safety by default.** We protect creators' accounts, footage and privacy as if they were our own.
4. **Always a next step.** No one leaves a conversation with us stuck.

## Positioning

For new and growing creators who want to spend their time creating, acTS is the friendly tech studio that sets you up, clips your content and keeps you safe online. Unlike generic IT help or faceless editing apps, we know creator culture, speak plainly and stick around after setup.

## Brand inspirations

- **Elgato:** clean creator-tech product look, dark interfaces, gear that feels pro but accessible.
- **Proton:** calm, trustworthy, privacy-minded voice, and confident use of purple.
- **Guerrilla:** bold, striking visuals with a strong point of view.

**Not like Apple:** we never feel exclusive, expensive or minimal-to-the-point-of-cold. We don't hype ("magical", "revolutionary"). We meet creators where their budget is.
