## Service blurbs

**Starter setups**
New to streaming? We'll build you a setup that looks and sounds great without blowing your budget. You get a parts list matched to your goals, a setup session, and help when you go live for the first time.

**Clipping**
Turn your long videos and podcasts into short clips that people actually watch. We find the best moments, add captions and send them ready to post to TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

**Safety and security packs**
Keep your accounts, your footage and your privacy safe. Our easy guides cover passwords, two-step login, scam DMs and keeping your location private.

**Ads for creators**
Short ads for your brand or your sponsors, made to fit your style and your audience.

**Content analytics**
A clear monthly report on what's working, what isn't and what to post next. No spreadsheets, no jargon.
