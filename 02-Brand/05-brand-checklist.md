# 05 · Brand Checklist

Run every piece of acTS output (post, email, guide, ad, invoice, web page) through this list before it goes out. Any "no" means fix it first.

## Name

- [ ] The brand is written **acTS** (never ACTS, Acts or AcTS)
- [ ] The full name, **All Creative Technology Solutions (acTS)**, appears on first mention in documents and web pages

## Voice

- [ ] It says "we", not "I" (unless Jess is signing personally)
- [ ] It sounds calm and kind, with one clear recommendation where one is needed
- [ ] It uses Australian spelling and AUD prices
- [ ] Any tech term is explained in plain words the first time
- [ ] There are no hype words (magical, revolutionary, game-changing) and no corporate jargon
- [ ] There is at most one exclamation mark, and at most one emoji (none in emails or documents)
- [ ] It ends with a clear next step
- [ ] If we can't help, it points them to someone who can

## Visual

- [ ] Only palette colours are used (see `03-visual-identity.md`)
- [ ] Solid Signal Green is on one key thing only (usually the main button), and never as text on white. Small green status marks (ticks, "Done" chips) are fine
- [ ] Status colours (Signal Green, Caution Amber, Alert Red) always come with an icon and a word, never colour alone, and switch to Deep Green, Deep Amber or Deep Red on light backgrounds
- [ ] Text and background pairs are from the accessible pairings table
- [ ] Headings in Space Grotesk, body in Inter (or Arial as the fallback)
- [ ] The logo has clear space, isn't stretched and has no effects added
- [ ] Imagery is real creator setups, moody lighting, nothing glossy-corporate

## Promise

- [ ] Would a new creator feel supported and heard after reading this?
- [ ] Is it easy, useful and intuitive?
