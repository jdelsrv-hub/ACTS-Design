# 02 · Voice and Tone

## Our voice in one line

A calm, kind Aussie tech crew that knows its stuff, talks like a mate, and writes like a big brand.

## Voice rules

1. **Say "we".** We are a small studio. Use "we", "us", "our team". Use "I" only when Jess signs a personal message.
2. **Casual Aussie, not slang-heavy.** Natural Australian English: "no worries", "heaps", "sorted", "reckon", "keen". One light Aussie touch per message is plenty. No "fair dinkum", "crikey" or forced slang.
3. **Australian spelling.** colour, organise, optimise, centre, licence (noun), program (software). Dates as 19 October 2026. Prices in AUD with "inc. GST" when relevant.
4. **Kind first.** Acknowledge how the creator feels before fixing. Never make them feel silly for not knowing.
5. **Calm expert.** Short, confident statements. Explain the why in one line. No panic, even in problems.
6. **A bit bold.** Give a clear recommendation when asked. "We'd go with X" beats "there are many options".
7. **Easy to read.** Short sentences (under 20 words where possible). One idea per paragraph. Use lists for steps.
8. **Plain words over jargon.** If a tech term is needed, explain it in a few words the first time: "bitrate (how much data your stream sends each second)".
9. **Always a next step.** End with what happens next or what they can do now.
10. **Honest limits.** If we can't help, say so and point them to who can.

## Tone by situation

| Situation | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Social posts | Bold, upbeat, punchy | "Your stream deserves better than a laptop mic. Here's a $150 setup that sounds pro." |
| First enquiry | Warm, welcoming, curious | "Hey! Thanks for reaching out. Tell us a bit about what you make and we'll sort out the rest." |
| Explaining tech | Calm, clear, step by step | "First, we'll check your upload speed. That tells us how high your stream quality can go." |
| Something broke | Calm, reassuring, fast | "No stress, this is fixable. Let's get you back live. Can you send a screenshot of the error?" |
| Pricing and quotes | Straight, transparent | "The Starter Setup is $X inc. GST. That covers the parts list, setup call and a week of support." |
| Saying no | Kind, helpful | "That one's outside what we do, but we know someone who's great at it. Here's their link." |
| Security training | Serious but not scary | "Most hacks start with one reused password. Here's a 10-minute fix." |

## Words we use

sorted · set up · easy · simple · clear · keen · heaps · no stress · we've got you · let's · next step · creator · your setup · your content · budget-smart · worth it · safe · supported

## Words we avoid

| Avoid | Why | Use instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Magical, revolutionary, game-changing | Hype, Apple-like | Easy, better, faster |
| Synergy, leverage, solutions-driven | Corporate jargon | Work together, use, help |
| Users, customers (in copy) | Cold | Creators, you |
| Just, simply (when it isn't simple) | Can feel patronising | Leave it out |
| Cheap | Sounds low quality | Budget-friendly, affordable, great value |
| Guys | Not inclusive | Everyone, folks, creators |
| Obviously, as you know | Makes people feel behind | Leave it out |
| Exclamation marks everywhere | Loses calm | One per message, max |
| Emoji overload | Loses big-brand polish | 0 to 1 emoji in DMs and social; none in emails or documents |

## Before and after

**Too technical** → **acTS**
> Before: "Your encoder settings are misconfigured; switch from x264 to NVENC and set CBR at 6000 kbps."
> After: "Your PC is working harder than it needs to. We'll switch it to use your graphics card for streaming, which makes things smoother. It takes about 5 minutes."

**Too corporate** → **acTS**
> Before: "acTS delivers end-to-end technology solutions to optimise creator workflows."
> After: "You create. We sort the tech. Setups, clips, ads and online safety for creators."

**Too casual** → **acTS**
> Before: "omg ur stream lagging?? lol happens to the best of us 😂😂 hmu"
> After: "Stream lagging? It happens to heaps of creators and it's usually an easy fix. Send us a message and we'll take a look."

**Too pushy** → **acTS**
> Before: "Don't miss out! Book our premium package NOW before prices go up!"
> After: "Ready to level up your setup? Book a free 15-minute chat and we'll tell you exactly what's worth buying."

## Writing about the brand name

- First mention in a document or page: **All Creative Technology Solutions (acTS)**. After that: **acTS**.
- In casual social posts and DMs, **acTS** alone is fine.
- Always write it **acTS**: lowercase a and c, uppercase T and S. Never "ACTS", "Acts" or "AcTS".
- At the start of a sentence, keep it **acTS**.
