data/words.txt is derived from the English 2018 frequency list in hermitdave/FrequencyWords:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/master/content/2018/en/en_50k.txt

Filter: kept lines whose word matches ^[a-z]+('[a-z]+)?$ (lowercase ASCII letters with at
most one apostrophe part), in the original order, and stopped at the first 20,000.
Each line is "word count".
