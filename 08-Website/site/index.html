FAQ

Questions creators ask us

How much does it cost?

Every creator's goals and budget are different, so we start with a free 15-minute chat. After that we send a clear quote in AUD before any work starts.

Do I need to be in Sydney?

No. We're based in Sydney and do most work online, over video calls and screen sharing. In-person setups are available around Sydney.

I'm brand new to streaming. Is that ok?

Yes, heaps of the creators we help are just starting out. We'll explain each step as we go, with no jargon and no silly questions.

Which platforms do you work with?

TikTok, Twitch, YouTube, Instagram and podcasts. If you're somewhere else, ask us anyway.

What if you can't help with my problem?

We'll tell you straight away and point you to someone who's great at it. You won't be left stuck.
