Privacy policy

Your information, looked after

Last updated 23 October 2026

Draft for Jess to review before launch. Have a professional check it if anything is unclear: this draft isn't legal advice.

All Creative Technology Solutions (acTS) is a sole-trader business run by Jess in Sydney, NSW (ABN 51 266 261 094). This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website, our free tools and our work with creators, and how we look after it. In short: we collect only what we need to help you, we never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it.

What we collect

  • When you contact us or book a chat: your name, your email and what you tell us about your content and what you need.
  • When we work with you: what the job needs, such as your channel names, gear details, footage you send for clipping, quotes, invoices and payment records.
  • When you visit our website: visitor stats that don't identify you, like how many people visited and which pages they read. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies to follow you around. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address (your internet connection's address) when a page loads.
  • When you use acTS Tools: most tools run in your browser, so your files stay on your device and we never see them. See "acTS Tools" below for the exceptions.

We never ask for your passwords. If a job needs access to your accounts, we ask you to add us through the platform's own team or manager settings. That way you stay in control and can remove us at any time.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to you and run the chat you booked
  • To quote, do the work and invoice you
  • To keep the records the law requires, like tax records
  • To improve our website and services, using stats that don't identify you

We only email you about your enquiry or your job. We won't add you to a mailing list unless you ask to join one.

Who we share it with

We never sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the services we use to run acTS, and only what each one needs:

  • Website hosting and visitor stats: Cloudflare
  • Contact form: Tally
  • Bookings: Cal.com or Calendly
  • Email: our email provider
  • AI writing tools: Anthropic (see below)

Some of these services store information outside Australia, for example in the United States. We pick well-known services with strong security, and their own privacy policies apply too. We'll also share information if the law requires it.

acTS Tools

  • Most tools work entirely in your browser. Your files aren't uploaded, and we can't see them.
  • Some tools, like the background remover and audio to text, download an AI model to your browser the first time you use them. The model runs on your device, so your files still stay with you.
  • The tools load some code and AI models from content delivery networks (services that send files quickly from servers near you), such as jsDelivr. Like any website, they see your IP address when they send the files.
  • The AI writing tools send the text you type to our AI service. It uses Claude, made by Anthropic, to write the result. We don't keep your text or the result, and Anthropic handles them under its own terms and privacy policy. Please don't type private details, like passwords or bank details, into the AI writing tools.
  • To stop misuse, our AI service counts the requests from each IP address for a short time. We don't use it to work out who you are.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't go ahead: deleted after 12 months.
  • Client records, quotes and invoices: 5 years, because the ATO requires business records to be kept that long.
  • Footage you send for clipping: deleted 60 days after the job ends, unless you ask us to keep it.

How we keep it safe

We use strong, unique passwords and two-step login on every account we run, work through platform team settings instead of shared passwords, and remove our access when a job ends. If something goes wrong with your information that's likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you as soon as we can and explain what we're doing about it.

Your choices

  • Ask to see the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct it
  • Ask us to delete it (we'll keep only what the law requires)
  • Remove our access to your accounts at any time

Contact us and we'll reply within 30 days.

Under 18?

Please ask a parent or guardian to get in touch with you before you book a chat or send us anything.

Questions or worries

Get in touch through our contact form (our hello@ email is coming soon). We follow the Australian Privacy Principles as good practice, and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner has more about your privacy rights.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For bigger changes, we'll email the creators we're working with.
