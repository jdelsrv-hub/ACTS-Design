## What Jess needs to check

1. **Walk the menu paths on your own phone and computer.** We couldn't open the apps themselves, so paths come from official help pages and search results. These are the lowest-confidence ones:
   - **Twitch:** two-step login setup (Twitch's help pages wouldn't load; based on Twitch's own search snippets plus third-party guides), whether Twitch shows backup codes, and passkey support (not confirmed).
   - **TikTok:** where the passkey option sits, and there's no confirmed menu for connected apps (the pack says so).
   - **Instagram:** Meta is renaming Accounts Center to "Meta Account" through April 2027, so labels may differ. The Meta Business Suite steps (Settings, People, Partial access) are from Meta's guide title and third-party mirrors.
   - **TikTok Business Center** "free to set up": widely understood, but the official page didn't say.
   - **OBS** (Settings, Advanced, Stream Delay; Stop Streaming discard delay) and **Windows** Do not disturb options: from our knowledge and OBS developer notes, not an official manual.
   - **Android** location paths vary by phone; the pack says so.
   - **Police Assistance Line 131 444** and **accce.gov.au**: from our knowledge, not re-checked today.
2. **Affiliate and link placeholders.** Each has "Ad: we earn a commission if you buy through these links, at no extra cost to you." right beside it, and the pack opens with the standard disclosure line.
   - `[affiliate: Proton Pass]` in Part 1 (password manager) and Part 4 (email aliases). Proton is a confirmed program (30%). Bitwarden's free plan is named first in Part 1.
   - `[link: acTS web page for security keys]` in Part 1. No Amazon links go in the pack (Amazon AU bans them in PDFs). That web page holds the YubiKey and Titan links. If the page ends up with no affiliate links, delete the "Ad:" sentence next to it.
   - 1Password, Bitwarden and YubiKey are named on merit with no link (no program open to us).
   - If Proton isn't joined before launch, delete the Proton placeholders (keep the product name) and the "Ad:" lines. If no affiliate links remain anywhere, remove the disclosure at the top too, so it stays true.
3. **Considered, not placed:** Bitdefender Security for Creators (confirmed program, 20%). We haven't been able to check that it's worth US$119 or more a year to a small creator, or what it adds over the free steps in Part 1. It stays out until someone tests it. Proton VPN is also left out: a VPN doesn't stop the threats this pack covers.
4. **Placeholders to fill:** `[website]` and `[email]` in the closing section and checklist footer; `[XX] pages` in the listing after design.
