## Description

Most creator hacks start the same way: a reused password, a fake sponsorship "contract", or a link in a "copyright strike" email. Most doxxing starts with small clues, like a parcel label or the view from a window.

This pack walks you through closing those doors, one platform at a time. Every step tells you where to tap and links to the platform's official help page, so you're never guessing. It's written by All Creative Technology Solutions (acTS), a Sydney studio that handles the tech side for creators.

A few links inside earn us a commission. Each one is clearly marked as an ad, and we always name a free option.

## What's inside

- **Part 1: Lock down your accounts.** Password managers, passkeys, two-step login that beats text-message codes, backup codes and recovery details. Includes a platform guide for Google and YouTube, Twitch, Instagram and TikTok, with official links.
- **Part 2: Give your team access without sharing passwords.** YouTube channel permissions, Twitch roles, Meta Business Suite, and what TikTok offers.
- **Part 3: Spot the scams aimed at creators.** Red flags and what to do for malware "contracts", fake strikes and verification offers, brand impersonation, recovery scammers, and giveaway and collab scams.
- **Part 4: Stop doxxing.** Ten location clues to check, removing location from photos, business email, phone and PO Box, domain privacy, cleaning up search results, and keeping kids safe on camera.
- **Part 5: Stream safety.** OBS scenes and sources, a privacy scene, stream delay, autofill and notifications, plus a 5-minute pre-stream check.
- **Part 6: If you get hacked.** A first-hour plan, official recovery links, how to report impersonation, and Australian help contacts.
- **Part 7: A one-page checklist to print.**

## FAQs

**I'm not technical. Will I be able to follow it?**
Yes. It's written for creators, not IT people. Each step says where to tap, and links to the platform's own help page in case your screen looks different. You can work through it in short sessions over a week.

**I'm not in Australia. Is it still useful?**
Yes. Parts 1 to 5 work anywhere, and the official recovery and reporting links work worldwide. Part 6 lists Australian help contacts. Outside Australia, use your country's official cybercrime or scam reporting service.

**What happens when the apps change their menus?**
They change often. That's why every step links to the official help page, which always shows the current version. The pack shows the date we last checked everything.
